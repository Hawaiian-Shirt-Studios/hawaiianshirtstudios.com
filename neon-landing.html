Competence under calm.

Neon Landing starts near-silent and nearly empty. The first ninety seconds feel like a screensaver. Drag from an aircraft to trace where it should fly. Send each plane to the runway, each helicopter to the helipad, always matching its color. Stack them at different altitudes so they pass safely over one another, and steer clear of mountains and restricted zones.

Pressure arrives so gradually you can’t name the moment it started. And when the airspace finally fills, what you’ve made is a dense, glowing lattice of every path you ever drew. It is an ambient routing puzzle you leave feeling like an expert, not a juggler.

  • Draw-a-path controls, built for one thumb
  • Match each aircraft to the pad wearing its color
  • Route around mountains and restricted airspace
  • Calm by default, tension by accumulation
  • No ads, no subscription, just a one-time unlock
  • Native on iPhone & iPad
