Competence under calm.

Neon Landing starts near-silent and nearly empty. The first ninety seconds feel like a screensaver. Drag from an aircraft to trace where it should fly. Send each plane to the runway, each helicopter to the helipad, always matching its color. Stack them at different altitudes so they pass safely over one another, and steer clear of mountains and restricted zones.

Pressure arrives so gradually you can’t name the moment it started, and the whole game is keeping a calm head as the airspace fills. It is an ambient routing puzzle you leave feeling like an expert, not a juggler.

Support & FAQ

Purchases & unlocking

I bought the unlock but the game is still locked.

First, make sure you are signed in with the Apple ID you used to buy it. Then open Settings and tap "Restore Purchases." If it stays locked, email us and we will sort it out.

I bought it on one device. How do I unlock it on another device or a new phone?

Sign in to the other device with the same Apple ID, open Neon Landing, then go to Settings and tap "Restore Purchases." Your unlock carries over at no extra cost.

Does the unlock work for my family?

Yes. "Unlock All Features" is Family Sharing enabled, so one purchase covers everyone in your Family Sharing group.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, and request the refund there.

Progress & iCloud

Will my progress sync between my iPhone and iPad?

Yes. Levels, best scores, and trophies sync through iCloud, as long as both devices use the same Apple ID.

My progress did not sync, or I lost it. What do I do?

Check that iCloud is turned on and that both devices share the same Apple ID, then reopen the game and give it a moment to catch up. If it still looks wrong, email us.

If I delete and reinstall, do I lose everything?

No. With iCloud on and the same Apple ID, your progress comes back after you reinstall.

How do I reset my progress?

Open Settings and tap "Reset all progress," then confirm. It clears completed levels, best scores, and trophies on this device and in iCloud, but keeps your settings. The button appears once you have cleared 100 levels.

How to play

How do I play, and how do I land a plane?

Drag your finger from an aircraft to draw the path it should fly. Send each plane to a pad or runway of the matching color, and it will land.

Why will a plane not land?

A plane can only land on a pad or runway of its own color. Draw the path to the pad that matches the plane.

How do I stop two planes from crashing?

Tap a plane to change its altitude band. Two planes at different altitudes can cross over each other safely.

How do I make a plane wait?

Draw a loop into its path. The plane will hold and circle until you are ready for it.

How do I unlock more levels?

The free version includes a 30-level sampler. "Unlock All Features" opens all 999 levels, endless Keep Playing after a win, and full Custom mode.

The game is too hard. Is there an easier way?

Yes. In Settings you can raise the number of Lives (how many hits a run survives), up to three. With the unlock you can choose unlimited lives for a no-fail game, or drop into Zen for a calm, endless board.

Settings & accessibility

How do I turn off the music or the sound effects?

Open Settings and turn off the "Music" toggle or the "Sound Effects" toggle. You can switch either one on or off any time.

I cannot tell the colors apart.

Turn on colorblind shapes in Settings. It adds a symbol to each color, so you can match by shape as well as by color.

What are Lives?

Lives are how many hits a run survives before game over. Free players can set 1, 2, or 3 lives in Settings, and the unlock adds unlimited lives for a gentle, no-fail way to play.

Modes

What is the difference between Play, Custom, Zen, and Daily?

Play is the main level ladder. Custom lets you build your own board (part of the paid unlock), Zen is a calm endless board, and Daily is one shared puzzle each day. Zen and Daily are free.

What is the Daily, and when does it reset?

The Daily is a single puzzle that everyone plays on the same day. A new one arrives each day.

Technical & other

The app crashes, freezes, or the audio stopped.

Close the app fully and reopen it, and if needed restart your device. If the trouble continues, email us with your device model and iOS version.

Is there an Android version?

There is no Android or web version. Neon Landing is built for iPhone and iPad, and it also runs on Apple silicon Macs (M1 and later) as the iPad app.

How do I contact you or request a feature?

We would love to hear from you. Email with questions, bugs, or ideas any time.
