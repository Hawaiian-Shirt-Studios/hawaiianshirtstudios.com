Meet Approach

Our first game

Approach

Coming soon to iOS

You are the calm hand above a small, beautiful airspace. Draw flight paths with your finger, stack aircraft at different altitudes so they pass safely over one another, and land everything.

It starts near-silent and nearly empty. The first ninety seconds feel like a screensaver, and pressure arrives so gradually you can’t name the moment it started. Every session leaves behind a glowing lattice of every path you ever drew.

  • Ambient, session-based strategy
  • Draw-a-path controls, built for one thumb
  • No ads. No subscription. No pressure.
  • Native on iPhone & iPad
